Students get a one-time sign-in link by email. Everyone else goes to a secure Stripe checkout, then gets the same link. Already set a password? Sign in at chat.dirk.it.

What models are behind it?

A curated set of open frontier models — Kimi K2, DeepSeek, Qwen and others — routed through our own gateway. "Auto" picks the best one for your prompt.

How much can I use?

Roughly $2 of model spend a week, refilled automatically. That's hundreds of long conversations; if you hit the ceiling before the refill, the workspace tells you.

Is my data private?

Conversations live in our own database, not a vendor's. Model providers see requests but no account. You can turn memory off per account.

I paid for a course — why a separate sign-in?

The course app and the AI workspace are different systems. Same email, two logins. Your membership unlocks both; cancelling the course ends both.
