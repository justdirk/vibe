Legal, Equity, and Co-Founders: Building on Solid Ground

What investors are actually buying, when raising is right and when it's a mistake, how the process really runs, and what your deck has to do in the first ninety seconds.

Questions

Who is this for?

First-time founders, operators who want to go out on their own, and anyone who wants to think like a builder. No technical or finance background is assumed — the parts that need numbers are taught from zero.

Do I need an idea before I start?

It helps, but no. Masterclasses 1–3 are largely about finding and sharpening one, and every mission works on whatever idea you're holding at the time — including one you replace in week two.

Is this theory or practice?

Every masterclass ends with a mission applied to your idea, and Vault accumulates the output. By masterclass 11 you're rehearsing a pitch that exists because you built it, not because you downloaded a template.

How much time does it take?

About twenty focused minutes a day for the lessons; the missions take longer because they're real work on your own company. Everything is unlocked from day one — go at whatever pace your life allows.

What's actually included in the price?

$129 today covers your first 3 months of everything: all 14 masterclasses in the app, Vault, and the AI sparring tools. After 3 months your membership continues at $20/month — cancel anytime.

What happens to my documents if I cancel?

The plan, the financial story and the deck are yours. Export them before your membership ends and they're yours to keep — your company's documents shouldn't live behind my paywall.

Will this get me funded?

No course can promise that, and you should distrust any that does. What this does is make you fundable: a real problem, a defensible model, numbers you can defend, and a pitch you've already been interrupted during.

How is this different from an accelerator?

An accelerator gives you money, a network and a deadline, and takes equity. This gives you the curriculum and the documents, takes no equity, and costs $129. They're not substitutes — this is what makes you ready to apply to one.
