Delete your Dirk It account

Dirk It · operated by Exciting Ventures alimentos ltd

You can permanently delete your Dirk It account and all data associated with it at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

Open Dirk It → Profile → Settings → Delete Account → confirm. Your account is removed right away.

Or request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email mail@dirk.it from the address you signed up with, subject “Delete my Dirk It account”. We process email requests within 7 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Your login (email, name, language), all learning data (lesson progress, XP, streaks, review history, quiz answers), mission submissions including any photos you uploaded, in-app usage events, and any content cached on our servers for your account.

What is kept, and for how long

Purchase and subscription records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat are governed by those providers; we keep only anonymised transaction references required for accounting and fraud prevention, for the period required by law (up to 5 years in Brazil / the EU). Server backups that may contain your data are rotated within 30 days. Nothing else is retained.
