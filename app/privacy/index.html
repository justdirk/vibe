Privacy Policy

Dirk It · Effective July 7, 2026 · Updated September 7, 2026

Dirk It (“we”, “us”) is a learning app operated by Exciting Ventures alimentos ltd. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights.

Data we collect

Account: your email address, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), first name (optional), and language preference.

Learning data: lesson progress, XP, streaks, spaced-repetition review history, and quiz answers — used to run the core features of the app.

Mission submissions: photos or other material you voluntarily submit to complete missions. They are used to verify the mission (including automated AI review), are stored on our infrastructure, and are not shared publicly.

Usage analytics: in-app events (e.g. lesson started/completed) tied to your account, used to improve the product. We do not sell your data and we do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Payments: purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google and RevenueCat. We never see your card details. We receive purchase and subscription status only.

Offline storage on your device

To make the app usable without an internet connection, course content you have opened and your recent progress are cached locally on your device. Progress made offline is synced to your account when you reconnect. This cache lives only on your device and is removed when you delete the app or your account.

Processors we use

Supabase (database, authentication, hosting), Cloudflare (media storage and delivery), RevenueCat (subscription management), Apple / Google (payments), and AI providers that process lesson content and mission verification. Each processor receives only the data needed for its function.

Retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data anytime in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including GDPR in the EU/EEA and LGPD in Brazil), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data, and to object to processing. Contact us to exercise them.

Children

Dirk It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes & contact

We will post any changes to this policy here. Questions or requests: mail@dirk.it.
